LunarTune — plain text post
===========================

This file is here to show that .txt works too.

Plain text is shown exactly as written, in a monospace block, with no markdown
interpreted. Use it for things like patch notes, a short release summary, or any
text you do not want reformatted.

Notes about the app in general:

- News lives in this folder and is read over the network, never bundled.
- The list sorts by the date in the file name, newest first.
- Files starting with . or _ are ignored.
